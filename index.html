Questions

Frequently Asked

What is Bloomfield BCCC and who is it for?

Bloomfield BCCC is an open community for anyone who wants to learn, create, and connect. Whether you're a student, professional, hobbyist, or just curious — you belong here. We welcome all skill levels, backgrounds, and disciplines.

How much does membership cost?

Our core membership is completely free. Some premium events and workshops may have a small fee to cover materials and venue costs, but the majority of our programming is open to all at no charge.

How often do you host events?

We host events weekly — typically 2-3 per week ranging from casual coffee chats to structured workshops and monthly flagship events. Check our events section or join our mailing list to stay updated.

Can I host my own event or workshop?

Absolutely! We actively encourage members to lead sessions on topics they're passionate about. Reach out through our contact form or speak with any organizer at an event. We'll help you plan, promote, and run your session.

I'm new to the area. Will I fit in?

100%. Many of our most active members started exactly where you are. We have dedicated "welcome events" for newcomers, and our community culture prioritizes inclusivity. You'll feel at home from day one.
