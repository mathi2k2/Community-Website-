Bloomfield Cricket Club brings together cricket lovers of all levels in Winnipeg, Manitoba. From competitive premier division matches to recreational T20 games, we play hard and build community.

Questions

Frequently Asked

What is Bloomfield Cricket Club?

Bloomfield CC is a cricket club based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, competing in the Manitoba Cricket Association leagues. We have three teams: Bloomfield CC (Premier Division), Bloomfield Knights (Division 2), and Bloomfield All Stars (Recreational T20).

How do I join the club?

Fill out the registration form below or come to one of our practice sessions. We welcome players of all skill levels — from beginners to experienced cricketers. Our recreational team is a great entry point for newcomers.

Where do you play matches?

Our home ground is Labarriere Park in Winnipeg. MCA league matches are played across various grounds in the Winnipeg area. Practice sessions are held regularly — check with us for the latest schedule.

Do you offer softball and hardball cricket?

Yes. Our Premier Division and Division 2 teams play hardball cricket, while the Recreational T20 league uses a softball format — perfect for players who are newer to the game or prefer a lighter format.

How much does it cost to play?

Membership fees cover MCA registration and league costs. We keep fees affordable and accessible. Contact us for the current season's rates. Equipment can be provided for newcomers trying out the sport.
